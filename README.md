## 📊 PORTFOLIO PROJECTS

### 1. Logistics Operations Dashboard
[View project](https://github.com/christianah-portfolio/Logistics-Operation-Dashboard)

**Tools & Skills:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, data modelling, data cleaning, KPI design, data validation

**Project Goal:** Find where a trucking company loses time and money across deliveries, fleet, costs, and safety.

**Results:** Only 44.61% of deliveries were on time, and this was the same across all routes and months, which points to a company-wide issue. Fuel took about 36% of revenue ($0.78 per mile), and 38% of safety incidents were preventable.

### 2. MTN Customer Overview Dashboard
[View project](https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/mtn-customer-churn-dashboard)

**Tools & Skills:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX (DISTINCTCOUNT, AVERAGE, SUM), data cleaning, dashboard design

**Project Goal:** Understand customer behaviour, churn, revenue, data usage, and satisfaction for a telecom customer dataset.

**Results:** 146 of 496 customers (about 29%) had churned. The top reasons were better competitor offers and high call tariffs. The 1.5TB Yearly Broadband Plan earned the most revenue, and I recommended competitive pricing, better-value data plans, and more promotion of long-term plans.

### 3. Betting Addiction Among Nigerians Dashboard
[View project](https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/betting-addiction-excel-dashboard)

**Tools & Skills:** Excel, Power Query, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, slicers, KPI cards

**Project Goal:** Explore betting habits and addiction scores across 2,000 bettors in six Nigerian cities and six betting platforms.

**Results:** Ibadan had the highest average addiction score (5.72), Bet9ja users had the highest average weekly bet (13.49), and over 60% of respondents reported financial debt.
